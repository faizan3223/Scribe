About Scribe AI

            

                
Scribe AI is your everyday writing and thinking partner. Draft emails, summarize documents, brainstorm ideas, and get quick answers — all from one simple chat window.

                
What we offer

                
    
                    
  • Fast, clear AI chat for writing and research
    • 
                    
  • Voice input and spoken replies
    • 
                    
  • Image and file attachments
    • 
                    
  • Optional AI image & video generation (Pro / Ultra)
    • 
                

                
Our mission

                
We help people write faster and think clearer — without unnecessary complexity. Scribe is built for students, creators, teams, and anyone who works with words every day.
